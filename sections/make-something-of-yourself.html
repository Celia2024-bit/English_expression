make something of yourself

口语 / 通用

有所作为；混出个样子；让自己有所成就。否定常说 never made anything of himself，比 make nothing of yourself 更自然。

快速定位：让自己有所成就

He wants to make something of himself.
他想混出个样子来。
She left the village to make something of herself in the city.
她离开村子，想去城里闯出一番事业。
He never made anything of himself.
他一辈子没混出什么名堂。
