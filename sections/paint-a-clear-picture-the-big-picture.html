paint a clear / complete / rosy picture · the big picture

通用 / 搭配

picture 在这里指“对情况的整体描述或了解”。paint a clear (vivid) picture＝把情况描述清楚；get / have the complete picture＝掌握全貌；paint a rosy picture＝说得一片大好（反义 a bleak / grim picture）；the big picture＝全局；a high-level picture＝宏观概览（写代码、讲架构时常用）。

快速定位：描述全貌、概况或全局

The report paints a clear picture of how the project went wrong.
报告清楚地说明了项目是怎么出问题的。
We still don't have the complete picture.
我们还没掌握全貌。
The CEO painted a rosy picture of next year's growth.
CEO 把明年的增长说得一片大好。
Her review painted a glowing picture of the product.
（glowing 更常用于 review / report）她的评测把这款产品描绘得极好。
Let me give you a high-level picture of the architecture first.
我先给你讲一下架构的整体概况。
Don’t get lost in the details — keep the big picture in mind.
别陷进细节，要记着全局。
