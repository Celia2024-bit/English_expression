take an interest in sth / be interested in sth

通用 / 同义表达

都表示对某事感兴趣。take an interest in 强调主动投入关注、有行动（常加 active / keen / genuine）；be interested in 只说明“有兴趣”这一状态，不一定有行动。

快速定位：主动关注 vs 有兴趣

She's started to take an interest in politics.
她开始关注起政治来了。（主动）
Parents should take an active interest in their children’s education.
父母应该积极关注孩子的教育。（主动）
He never took much interest in school.
他从来不怎么关心学业。
I'm interested in learning more about it.
我有兴趣进一步了解。（状态）
