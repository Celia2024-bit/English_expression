born into money / born with a silver spoon in one's mouth

通用 / 比喻

born into money / a wealthy family＝出生在富裕家庭；born with a silver spoon in one’s mouth＝含着金汤匙出生（有时略带酸意）。法语：naître dans une famille aisée / riche；“cuillère d’argent”的说法是从英语借来的，不如英语常用；带贬义的“富二代”是 fils / fille à papa。

快速定位：出身富裕

She was born into a wealthy family.
她出生在一个富裕的家庭。
He was born into money, so he never had to worry about rent.
他生来就有钱，从来不用为房租发愁。
Not everyone is born with a silver spoon in their mouth.
不是每个人都含着金汤匙出生。
I wasn't born into money; I worked my way up.
我不是富二代，是一步步干上来的。
