change for the better / worse

通用

朝好的或坏的方向发生变化。 change for the worse 变得更坏 , turn for the worse 情况恶化 have a turn for the worse 非常常用的方法

快速定位：描述好转或恶化

His attitude changed for the worse.
他的态度变得更差了。
Things have changed for the better.
情况已经有所好转。
Things have taken a turn for the worse.
情况突然恶化了。
The economic situation in the region turned for the worse after the market drop
市场下跌后，该地区的经济进一步恶化
