be fascinated by / with sth

通用

对某事深深着迷，觉得特别有趣、新奇，比 be interested in 强烈得多。强度大致是：be interested in < take an interest in < be fascinated by < be obsessed with。注意：对人或外表“被吸引”更常用 be attracted to。

快速定位：深深着迷

She's fascinated by ancient languages.
她对古代语言很着迷。
I've always been fascinated by how the brain works.
我一直对大脑的运作方式着迷。
The kids were fascinated by the magic show.
孩子们被魔术表演迷住了。
It fascinates me how quickly children learn.
孩子学得那么快，让我觉得很神奇。
