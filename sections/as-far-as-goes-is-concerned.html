as far as ... goes / as far as ... is concerned / when it comes to / in terms of / speaking of

通用 / 同义表达

“就……而言”的几种说法：as far as ... goes 偏口语；as far as ... is concerned 常带个人立场；when it comes to 后接名词或动名词；in terms of 通用、略正式。speaking of 不限定范围，而是顺势换话题。

快速定位：限定讨论范围 / 转换话题（同义表达）

As far as the budget goes, we're in good shape.
就预算而言，我们的状况不错。
As far as I'm concerned, the matter is closed.
就我而言，这件事已经结束了。
When it comes to cooking, he's a natural.
说到做饭，他很有天赋。
In terms of salary, the offer is great.
就薪资来说，这个 offer 很不错。
Speaking of food, have you tried the new Thai place?
（换话题）说到吃的，你试过新开的那家泰餐吗？
